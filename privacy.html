Privacy policy

restic-backup is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

It reads files on that computer, encrypts them on the computer with restic, and writes the encrypted data to the owner's own Google Drive. It can access only the files it has created there (the drive.file scope).

No data is collected, sold, shared with or sent to anyone else. There is no server, analytics or tracking.

The owner can revoke its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
